Our story

Why we builtAureum.

A room worth showing up for, built by two founders who couldn't find one.

How it started

We couldn't find the room we wanted to be in.

Anna and Maroussia had sat through enough networking to know the feeling: a room full of people scanning for a VC, quietly deciding whether you're worth their time.

So we built the opposite. Drinks, coffee, dinners and masterclasses with one rule: people come to give as much as they take. Aureum is a non-profit, so nobody in the room is selling you anything.
