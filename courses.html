Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience with AI?

No. The first workshop is designed for people with little or no experience. For Claude Code, having used Claude or another AI assistant before is helpful, but you don't need to know programming.

I'm not technical. Is this really for me?

Yes. No coding is needed in any of the three workshops. We show every step first, then you do it yourself, and we help as soon as something doesn't work.

Why not just learn it on YouTube?

Videos show someone else's project. Here you work on your own business, with a trainer next to you when you get stuck, and you go home with something that already works.

I don't know what to build yet.

That's fine. Each workshop page lists examples, and we help you pick a realistic project on the day. Our trainers' own products, Spaartje, Far & Wide and Kalmly, show what's possible.

I don't have much time.

It's half a day for workshops 1 and 3, and one day for Claude Code. The point is to win hours back every week after that.

Do I need a paid Claude account?

Yes. We recommend Claude Pro for all workshops. For the full-day Claude Code workshop, we recommend Claude Max because building applications can use more of your available usage allowance. Your subscription is not included in the workshop ticket.

Do I need to bring anything?

A laptop, its charger and an idea, task or challenge from your business. We'll send you a preparation guide before the workshop.

Will I actually build something?

Yes. Every workshop includes practical exercises and a tangible result. You'll leave with a configured AI assistant and automation, an initial working application or prepared marketing assets, depending on the session.

Can I attend just one workshop?

Absolutely. Each workshop is designed to be useful on its own. The full programme is ideal if you want to develop all three skills.

What if I get stuck?

You won't stay stuck for long: our groups are small, so you get guidance during the workshop. Afterwards you have 30 days in a WhatsApp group with the trainers, plus an online Q&A after two weeks.

Is this suitable for my employees or team?

Yes. The programme is relevant to professionals who want to introduce practical AI workflows into their work. Many people ask their employer to pay: we can provide an invoice in your company's name.

Will I need to spend extra money on software?

Claude Pro is required. Some other tools have free plans or trials, while certain business uses may require paid subscriptions. We'll explain the requirements in advance. Advertising spend is never included in the workshop fee.

Where does it take place?

Central Amsterdam. We send you the exact address by email at least a week before the workshop, together with everything you need to prepare.

Ready to put AI to work?

You don't need to understand every AI tool on the market.

You need to know how to use the right ones to solve real problems, build useful things and grow your business.

Three practical workshops. Small groups. Skills you can use immediately.
