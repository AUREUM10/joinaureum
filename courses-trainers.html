Your trainers

Tatiana Maes

Tech founder, AI builder and enterprise technology advisor · All three workshops

Tatiana didn't start her career as a software developer. She learned to build software using AI and turned that skill into real products.

She created Spaartje, a Dutch grocery price comparison app that reached the top of the App Store's Food & Drink category, and co-founded Far & Wide, a technology company helping businesses understand and improve how they appear in AI-generated answers.

Alongside building her own products, Tatiana brings years of experience advising on enterprise technology. Her teaching approach is practical: less time explaining technical concepts, more time showing you how to make things work.

Maroussia Tyl

Co-founder of The Aureum Club, founder of WOGO Cocktail Walk · Workshop 03

Maroussia runs the marketing for two businesses of her own. She founded WOGO Cocktail Walk, a self-guided cocktail experience running in cities across the Netherlands, and The Aureum Club, the club behind this programme.

She writes, builds and manages her own Facebook and Instagram campaigns: the pixel, the audiences, the creative, the budget and the weekly read of what is actually working. Everything in this workshop is what she uses on her own accounts.

She also built Kalmly, a Meta ads tool for small businesses that gives one clear decision every morning.
