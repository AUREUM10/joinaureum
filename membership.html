Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to be a member to come?

No. Every evening is open at the regular ticket price via Luma. Members reserve first and pay less.

Is this for women or for men?

Both. Aureum started as a women's founder network and is now open to everyone building something in Amsterdam.

What does "by application" mean?

We read what you're building and ask one thing: would we seat you next to the people already in the room? Not revenue, not title.

What does it cost?

€100 a year, or €20 a month. Aureum is a non-profit: fees and tickets pay for rooms, tables, food and drinks. Nothing else.
