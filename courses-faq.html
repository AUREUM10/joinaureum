Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need any experience with AI?

No, and no coding either, in any of the three workshops. We show every step first, then you do it yourself, and we help as soon as something doesn't work. For Claude Code, having used Claude or another AI assistant before is helpful.

Why not just learn it on YouTube?

Videos show someone else's project. Here you work on your own business, with a trainer next to you when you get stuck, and you go home with something that already works.

I don't know what to build yet.

That's fine. Each workshop page lists examples, and we help you pick a realistic project on the day. Our trainers' own products, Spaartje, Far & Wide and Kalmly, show what's possible.

I don't have much time.

It's half a day for workshops 1 and 3, and one day for Claude Code. The point is to win hours back every week after that.

Do I need a paid Claude account?

Yes, Claude Pro, about €22 a month including VAT. You can cancel it after the programme. Claude Max is optional: it gives you more room on the full day of building, but Pro is enough. Your subscription is not included in the ticket.

Do I need to bring anything?

A laptop, its charger and an idea, task or challenge from your business. We'll send you a preparation guide before the workshop.

Will I actually build something?

Yes. Every workshop includes practical exercises and a tangible result. You'll leave with a configured AI assistant and automation, an initial working application or prepared marketing assets, depending on the session.

Can I attend just one workshop?

Absolutely. Each workshop is designed to be useful on its own. The full programme is ideal if you want to develop all three skills.

Is this suitable for my employees or team?

Yes. The programme is relevant to professionals who want to bring practical AI workflows into their work, and it works well for small teams.

Will I need to spend extra money on software?

Claude Pro is the only must. The other tools we use have free plans. Advertising spend is never included and always stays under your control.

What if I can't make it?

Transfer your seat to someone else at no cost, just tell us their name. Can't make one of the three dates in the full programme? Tell us as soon as you know and we'll look at it with you.

Can I get an invoice for my company?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email with the company name, address and VAT number and we'll send you an invoice in the company's name.

Where does it take place?

Central Amsterdam. We send you the exact address by email at least a week before the workshop, together with everything you need to prepare.
