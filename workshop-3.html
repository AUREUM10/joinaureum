Workshop 03 · AI Builder Program

AI for Ads & Marketing

Better content. Smarter campaigns. Less time spent on the repetitive work.

Running a business means constantly finding ways to attract new customers.

Social media posts. Product descriptions. Advertising campaigns. Content calendars. Creative ideas. Performance reports.

It's a lot to manage, particularly when marketing isn't your full-time job.

AI can make that work considerably easier. But simply asking it to write an Instagram post isn't much of a strategy.

In this workshop, you'll learn how to build a practical AI-powered marketing workflow around your business, your audience and your goals.

From creating content that actually sounds like your brand to preparing Facebook and Instagram campaigns and understanding their performance, you'll work on your own marketing throughout the session.

What you’ll learn

01

Teach AI your brand

Your business has its own voice, personality and customers. Your marketing should reflect that.

Learn how to give Claude the right information about your brand, audience, services and offers.

You'll create a reusable brand setup that helps AI produce content that's consistent with your business, rather than generic marketing copy.

02

Create content without starting from scratch

Plan your content around your customers and business goals, instead of wondering what to post every morning.

Learn how to generate ideas, write social media posts, prepare product descriptions and create visuals.

We'll also explore practical AI workflows for short-form video and adapting content for different channels.

03

Build your first AI-assisted ad campaign

Learn how to approach advertising on Facebook and Instagram.

We'll cover the essentials: choosing an objective, defining your audience, preparing ad creatives, deciding on a budget and setting up a campaign in Meta Ads Manager.

You'll see how AI can help with the research, messaging and creative process while you stay in control of the decisions and spending.

04

Understand what's working

Running an ad is one thing. Knowing whether it's delivering results is another.

We'll explain the metrics that matter, how to interpret campaign performance and how to decide what to improve.

You'll also see how AI can help analyse results and prepare clear, actionable reports.

05

Automate part of your marketing

Bring the pieces together into a repeatable workflow.

Learn how to prepare content ahead of time, generate regular marketing reports and use automation tools to reduce manual work.

We'll demonstrate practical examples you can adapt for your business.

What you’ll leave with

By the end of the afternoon, you'll have:

  • An AI workspace configured for your brand
  • A reusable brand voice guide
  • A content calendar for the coming month
  • A week of prepared social media content
  • A first Facebook or Instagram advertising campaign prepared for launch
  • A practical AI marketing workflow you can continue using

You'll leave understanding not only how to create marketing materials faster, but how to make better decisions about what you publish and promote.
